# Homepage font sources

## Karrik

Designed by Jean-Baptiste Morizot and Lucas Le Bihan; distributed by Velvetyne.
Original, unmodified regular and italic WOFF2 files from:
https://gitlab.com/phantomfoundry/karrik_fonts

License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, included in `karrik-OFL.txt`.

## Inclusive Sans

Designed by Olivia King and the Inclusive Sans project authors.
Original, unmodified upright and italic variable WOFF2 files from:
https://github.com/LivKing/Inclusive-Sans

License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, included in `inclusive-sans-OFL.txt`.

## Geist and Geist Mono (used by `blog/gepa-edits-weights`)

Designed by Vercel with Basement Studio. Variable TrueType files from the Google Fonts repository,
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/geist and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/geistmono,
converted to WOFF2 with fontTools and not otherwise changed.

License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, included in `geist-OFL.txt` and `geistmono-OFL.txt`.
