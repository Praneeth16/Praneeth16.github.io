# Praneeth Paikray, personal site

Personal writing site of an AI solutions architect. Essays and visual explainers about building and evaluating AI systems, aimed at engineers and technical readers who already know the vocabulary and want the mechanism shown clearly.

- Readers arrive from a shared link, read one piece end to end, and leave. There is no product to sign up for.
- Each piece is self-contained: hand-written HTML or Astro, interactive where interaction teaches something, static where it does not.
- Claims are sourced. Interactive numbers are labelled as teaching examples unless they are measurements.
- No analytics, no cookies, no external font or script dependencies. Everything is self-hosted on GitHub Pages.

Surfaces:
- Home and article pages share the house layout (Fraunces, Spectral, DM Mono, paper and a blue accent).
- `blog/how-llms-are-built` is an Experience surface: a linear scroll story where the drawings lead and the chrome recedes. It has its own stylesheet and a drawing-sheet palette, but keeps the house body face and paper.
